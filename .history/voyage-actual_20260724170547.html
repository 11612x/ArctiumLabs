Result

Opening ROB is entered as layers (one per stem it came from) so FIFO carries across voyages. Closing ROB is taken from the last leg's arrival ROB. ROB dep/arr columns on legs follow the grades present here and in stems.
Bunkers can be stemmed at sea or in port. Each stem is counted in exactly one window — a stem timed exactly on an arrival or departure belongs to the port stay.
Exported workbooks carry live formulas — durations, speeds, consumptions, FIFO allocation, commissions and TCE all recalculate in Excel. Styling in the export is deliberately plain.
